lanes, IDM control, and seeded uniform speed variation. All physics is HighwayEnv.

No arbitrary Python is accepted. Do not change rewards, success checks or physics.

small, interpretable increase in structural complexity while retaining plausible
gaps. Explain which decision should become harder and why in hypothesis. If a
candidate was rejected, fix the reason. Avoid duplicates. Do not assume complexity
proves difficulty. A fixed controller failing does NOT prove impossibility.
Structural score is mean((vehicles-2)/16,(60-spacing)/42,speed_spread/7,
(300-merge_length)/160). Require an increase of at least 0.015 over last accepted
scenario. The goal is sequential interactive decision-making, not a static quiz.
